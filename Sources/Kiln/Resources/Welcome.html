The page is the editor. Your words belong right where you can see them.

Make yourself at home

Your agent got you most of the way there. Now make it yours. Click this sentence and change a few words — the design stays out of your way.

  • Click into any paragraph, heading, or list.
  • Put the cursor here and press Enter to add a bullet.
  • Select a few words, then try ⌘B or ⌘I.

No source pane. No rebuild. Just you and the finishing touches.

Bring your own page

Open a local HTML file with ⌘O. Kiln keeps its layout, styles, and nearby images. Lock editing with ⌥⌘L to read without a caret, or use the globe to open your saved page in a browser.
